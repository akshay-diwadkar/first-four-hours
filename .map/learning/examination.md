# Examination practice

- [Examiner](../../skills/learning/examiner/SKILL.md): start with how to present choices and HTML-only saves, identifying the examination, requirement research and the approximation rule, when a study plan is created, and Reason checkpoints.
- [Papers](../../skills/learning/examiner/references/papers.md): the paper-shape choice, fidelity to the real examination and its sourced rules, the four practice-set files including on-screen answering and a self-contained answer key, and the solve-and-audit checklist.
- [Paper runtime and its `paper-meta` timing data](../../skills/learning/examiner/assets/paper-runtime.html): the offline timed rehearsal every on-screen paper copies unchanged; start at its opening comment, which defines the question markup and the `paper-meta` timing data. [Its browser exercise, from `exercise`](../../tools/validation/exercise_paper_runtime.py) proves the behaviour in headless Chrome or Edge.
- [Marking](../../skills/learning/examiner/references/marking.md): recording attempts, the researched official basis or else an unofficial audited key for a supplied paper, applying each rule (levels and bands, consequential and negative marking) only where the basis or the examination's sourced rules state it, the aggregation basis, time as a diagnostic, grades only for official papers, diagnosis, and retries.
- [Practice records](../../skills/learning/examiner/references/practice-records.md): the `exams/<exam>/<date>-<kind>-<NN>/` layout, naming, the `scaffold` and `check` commands, the plain-language `START_HERE.html` page refreshed on every save, never-overwrite rule, and the correction route for a wrong key or scheme.
- [Examination study plan](../../skills/learning/examiner/references/study-planning.md): the Examiner-owned `examination-coverage` section of the shared `study-plan.html`, where diagnosed gaps are named for Tutor.

Papers never carry answers, and coverage is judged from marked attempts, never from papers delivered.
