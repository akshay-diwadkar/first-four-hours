# Routing and decision support

- [Router](../../skills/sensemaking/router/SKILL.md): use when the next capability is unclear or several stages need coordination; its output returns control rather than executing the route.
- [Reason](../../skills/sensemaking/reason/SKILL.md): consult for judgment checkpoints, a consequential claim, or an unresolved decision; follow the relevant decide, challenge, or ask branch, and its checkpoint trace for owner reports.
- [Ideate](../../skills/sensemaking/ideate/SKILL.md): research alternatives or causal paths when possibilities must be discovered and compared.

Routing selects capability stages. Reason advises without persisting an artifact or taking the owning stage's authority. Ideate produces a research-backed recommendation or next discriminator, with selection authority handled by its contract. These are different outputs, so a route, consultation, or recommendation is not itself implementation completion.
