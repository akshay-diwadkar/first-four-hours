# Project workflows

Navigate the project-facing capabilities by the stage of work and the handoff it needs.

- [Investigate and decide structure](investigation.md): locate defect diagnosis and architecture decisions before implementation planning.
- [Plan and execute](planning-and-execution.md): trace the sealed-plan boundary and its verification contracts.
- [Review and manage work](review-and-tickets.md): locate exact-change assessment and work-item persistence or selection.
- [Define a project](project-definition.md): settle a whole project's purpose, scope, and success before tickets, and publish it as one umbrella issue.
- [Project orientation](orientation.md): maintain the filesystem wiki and its required root guidance; not for project-file truth.
