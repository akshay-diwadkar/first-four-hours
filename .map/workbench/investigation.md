# Investigation and structural decisions

- [Diagnose](../../skills/workbench/diagnose/SKILL.md): start with current defects, risks, or improvement opportunities and follow its evidence and sealing guidance.
- [Architect](../../skills/workbench/architect/SKILL.md): start with boundaries, ownership, dependencies, or an unspecified architecture opportunity.
- [Blueprint](../../skills/workbench/blueprint/SKILL.md): continue when findings or structural decisions need a decision-complete implementation plan; use its architecture-intake link for an Architect handoff.

Diagnosis and architecture stop before implementation. Their findings and structural decisions provide inputs to planning; they do not settle the executor's file-level instructions. Follow each producer's own artifact contract instead of assuming their output workspaces are interchangeable.
