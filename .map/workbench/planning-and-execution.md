# Planning and execution

- [Blueprint](../../skills/workbench/blueprint/SKILL.md): enter the planning workflow and its intent, evidence, challenge, and sealing boundaries.
- [Blueprint artifact and commands](../../skills/workbench/blueprint/README.md): locate the prepared scaffold, the mechanical check/seal interface, and which branch reference owns each seal diagnostic.
- [Planning contract](../../skills/workbench/blueprint/references/planning-contract.md): the core rules every plan must meet: decision completeness, commands and evidence, code expression, step proof, and verification with single-statement completion conditions, with pointers to its executable-contract, Ideate, declared-text (including step-proof authoring), and Incomplete-gate branch references.
- [Adversarial challenge](../../skills/workbench/blueprint/references/challenge-and-rehearsal.md): the evidence-based challenge tier, the fresh challenger's pre-mortem and step-order walk, repair, requirement trace, and seal gate.
- [Elevated attack](../../skills/workbench/blueprint/references/elevated-attack.md): the full attack dimensions an elevated-tier challenger adds to the pre-mortem.
- [Construct](../../skills/workbench/construct/SKILL.md): enter execution with an existing sealed Blueprint, including resume and verification commands.
- [Execution contract](../../skills/workbench/construct/references/execution-contract.md): Construct's literal-execution and local-repair boundary, step proof, Atlas upkeep, and stop classifications.
- [Repository planning traps](../../REPO_VERSIONING.md): read the Planning traps section when changing the paired Blueprint/Construct machinery or authoring verification in this repository.

Blueprint owns implementation decisions; Construct applies the sealed result and reports contradictions to planning. A successful seal and a successful execution establish different things. For model-quality and mechanical-proof limits, follow Choosing models in the [Blueprint user page](../../docs/skills/blueprint.md). Editing these skills' guidance also couples to budgets and pinned rules; see [contribution and validation](../maintenance/contribution.md).
