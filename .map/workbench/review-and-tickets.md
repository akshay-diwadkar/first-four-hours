# Review and work items

- [Assess](../../skills/workbench/assess/SKILL.md): review an exact change against authority and base truth, with a revision-bound result and separate check command.
- [Ticket](../../skills/workbench/ticket/SKILL.md): choose persistence, selection, or explicitly requested persistence followed by selection, then follow only the relevant backend guidance.
- [Blueprint](../../skills/workbench/blueprint/SKILL.md): consume a plan-ready ticket scope or plan remediation of a supported finding.

Assessment does not approve or merge a change. Ticket persistence and selection have distinct authority: selection can prepare a planning handoff without granting publication or implementation authority. Neither capability automatically starts the next stage.
