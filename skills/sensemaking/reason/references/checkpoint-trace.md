# Checkpoint trace

Read this when an owner skill whose work requires Reason checkpoints writes its final or stop report, or before the final reply of judgment work no owner skill reports on, such as Reason used outside this suite.

In that report, briefly account for each required checkpoint: the before-approach consultation, each failure or direction-change consultation (or no trigger), each further checkpoint the owner skill names, and the final consultation. Mark each consulted, missed, or pending when not yet due, and say whether it ran in fresh or owner context; never put its result in the trace, though [Results](results.md) still requires stating any open disposition in the reply. Mark a checkpoint consulted only when its consultation ended with the visible result line naming the risk it tested; otherwise it is missed.

With no owner skill's report, the visible result lines are the trace: the final reply adds each missed or late-recovered checkpoint, never a full trace, beside any open disposition Results requires.

After a miss, consult and reconsider before proceeding, and report that recovery as late, never as timely compliance. Claim no readiness or completion while a required consultation is missing. Keep trace accounting in the report or that final reply: no new artifact, schema, or persisted state.
