# Elevated attack

An elevated challenger (see Tier in [Adversarial challenge](challenge-and-rehearsal.md)) also works through this.

Attack at least these dimensions and their examples; the examples are cues to look beyond, not a checklist to stop at:

- **Outcome:** a material decision left open or disguised as executor freedom; a requested outcome, preserved obligation, or affected consumer dropped by the plan's own non-goals; a requirement narrowed or reinterpreted without the user's decision; a compliant result that misses the request; unearned mechanisms such as tamper machinery inside a trusted local environment; inaccurate deliverable Exact text, such as docs or agent-loaded wording.
- **Problem:** wrong current-state, root-cause, owner, or seam assumptions; premises established only below the public entrypoint; repository checks that scan what new code may contain; undecided producible inputs or exit paths, including environment-probing states such as metadata present but unresolvable, unsupported layouts, and reserved names; outputs a consumer cannot act on or audit; completion or resume trusting a recorded status, success marker, or relationship an earlier run established instead of rereading its current source.
- **Scope:** omitted request clauses or imported architecture invariants; missing consumer, contract, or affected Atlas propagation; YAGNI violations; under-scoping disguised as minimality; unearned Exact text or any transcribed implementation.
- **Execution:** hidden choices or materially different faithful implementations; missing preservation; misleading or incomplete snippets; pseudocode settling the wrong branches or order; plan-internal contradictions, such as a later step or check relying on a Source fact an earlier step deletes or rewrites, or an instruction or declared text that makes later proof impossible, like a search check the plan's own Exact text would fail; unsafe ordering; missing compatibility, migration, or recovery; completion that requires replanning.
- **Verification:** proof that passes a plausible wrong result or mocks away the claimed operation; a gate that cannot pass on the current tree for a reason outside the planned diff; broad gates (beyond the planned diff) without a recorded current-tree result; a budget claimed to fit without recorded headroom; environment-sensitive gates or `Incomplete:` declarations, signatures, or omissions that break the [Incomplete gates](incomplete-gates.md) rule; unhandled applicable repository traps or foreseeable branches requiring replanning; executable conditions mislabeled as owner gates, or change obligations hidden there to skip their instruction; a `Run:` missing its executable, accepted arguments, prerequisites, or working directory; a Completion Condition that restates an instruction.
