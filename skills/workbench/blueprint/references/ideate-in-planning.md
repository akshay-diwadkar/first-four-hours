# Ideate in planning

Invoke `ideate` only when no viable solution is apparent or evidence cannot rank materially different viable directions, giving it the goal, constraints, and settled choices; its `.first-four-hours/ideations/` handoff is its own write. Act on its What to do section without replaying research: the direction is the user's choice and the details within it yours, so while the final choice is `yours to make`, get the user's selection, since a recommendation is never approval, and never seal on a `research limit reached` direction. Record the selection in the authoritative request before preparing; a later selection supersedes the workspace. Never cite the ideations workspace as Source.
