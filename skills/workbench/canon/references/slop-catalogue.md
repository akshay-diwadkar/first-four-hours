# Slop catalogue

Slop is generic sameness: design or copy that reads as a model's default output rather than a decision made for this product. Each entry names one pattern. A pattern is slop when it stands in for a decision; when a canon chose it deliberately, the canon records that as a named exception or amendment, and the entry stays. Craft defects such as broken spacing, misalignment, or uneven finish are never entries, and a product's own bans belong in its canon's `## Banned` list.

## Design

- **Gradient brand**: a purple-to-blue gradient as the main colour.
- **One sans for everything**: Inter or a system sans for every role.
- **Stock hero**: a centred headline, subhead, two buttons, and a blob or mockup.
- **Three feature cards**: an icon, a bold title, and two lines per card, three in a row.
- **Card soup**: every block a rounded, shadowed card.
- **Glass panels**: frosted panels over blurred blobs.
- **Icon per bullet**: emoji or generic line icons as decoration.
- **Neon dark**: dark mode with glowing accents as the premium default.
- **Pill everything**: pill buttons and badges, with a gradient primary.
- **Filler illustration**: 3D or isometric art unrelated to the content.
- **Scroll reveal**: every block fades and slides up.
- **Proof strip**: a "trusted by" logo row and a testimonial carousel.
- **Three-bullet slides**: a title plus three bullets on every slide.
- **Bolded lead-ins**: a bold phrase opens every bullet or paragraph.
- **Default charts**: library colours, gridlines, legend, and 3D.

## Copy

- **Era openers**: "In today's fast-paced world".
- **Hype verbs**: "Unlock", "Elevate", "Supercharge", "Seamless", "Effortless".
- **Tricolon taglines**: "Fast. Simple. Powerful."
- **Not-just-X**: "It's not just X, it's Y".
- **Empty intensifiers**: "cutting-edge", "robust", "game-changing".
- **Cheery microcopy**: "Oops! Something went wrong.", "You're all set!" with an emoji.
- **Stock closer**: "Ready to get started?"

## Project additions

`.canon/slop-additions.md` starts with `# Slop additions`, then one bullet per addition, oldest first, as `- YYYY-MM-DD: **Name**: the pattern. Reason: the user's call, in their words.`
